Vehicles and rates

Mileage rates

Your saved IRS mileage rates are used for deduction estimates on trips and reports. Changes here apply to your ledger.

{% include "_rates_table.html" %}

Vehicles

Trips can be assigned to a vehicle so the deduction can be broken out per vehicle on the annual report. Deactivating a vehicle removes it from the picker for new trip assignments but leaves it (and its name) on trips already using it.

{% include "_vehicles_table.html" %}

Odometer readings

Log a dashboard odometer value now and then to reconcile it against GPS- detected distance. This catches driving the tracker missed (dead zones, app not running) and supplies the total-miles figure the actual-expense comparison needs. It doesn't change the standard-mileage deduction shown elsewhere in the app.

{% include "_odometer_table.html" %}
