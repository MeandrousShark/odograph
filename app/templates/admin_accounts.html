Deletion, exports and backups

Scheduling deletion immediately disables login, tracking and sends. An enabled administrator can cancel during the 30-day grace period. Cancellation allows a new sign-in but never revives revoked sessions, proofs or tracking credentials.

The owner can export before deletion or after cancellation. Administrators cannot export another account's data. Portable exports cover ledger, rates and basic preferences; notification destinations, schedules and raw points are omitted. A complete server backup is a separate operator action.

Purge removes live data only. Historical encrypted backups may retain account data until the operator's backup retention expires. Restoring an older backup can reintroduce purged data and requires restore-time session and proof revocation.
